Audiovisual Works

Transmutation, 2025 — Two entities meet at the banks of a river. Without any dialogue, a surreal exchange takes place. Alternatice film shot in a digicam. Screened as part of the Alternative Filmmaking Workshop in The Commercial. Original score produced, mixed and mastered by The Animal, using an AE Modular synth (Tangible Waves) and digital post-processing in FL Studio (Image-Line).

Receptacle, 2023 — A series of drum patterns are created using an AE Modular synth (Tangible Waves). Our orchid reacts to the audio signals using TouchDesigner (Derivative).
