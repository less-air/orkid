Collection

Audiovisual Works

Transmutation, 2025 — Two entities meet at the banks of a river. Without any dialogue, a surreal exchange takes place. Alternatice film shot in a digicam. Screened as part of the Alternative Filmmaking Workshop in The Commercial. Original score produced, mixed and mastered by The Animal (Nicolás Rojas & Hazel Gardner), using an AE Modular synth (Tangible Waves) and digital post-processing in FL Studio (Image-Line).

The Animal, 2025 — Music video for the upcoming live performance project 'The Animal' (Nicolás Rojas and Hazel Gardner). Visuals made using an audioreactive network in TouchDesigner. Imported 3D model of a fossil sculpted in Blender. Music created with an AE Modular Synth and an Arturia Beatstep Pro MIDI controller. Mixing and mastering in FL Studio.

Aura Remix, 2024 — Visualiser for the track 'Aura - Less Air Remix' by 25midi from the EP 'Aura Remixes'. Visuals made using an audioreactive network in TouchDesigner. Original track produced by 25midi (Niklas Kleemann). Remixed using an AE Modular Synth and an Arturia Beatstep Pro MIDI controller. Mixing and mastering in FL Studio.

Mudlarking, 2023 — Music video for the track 'Mudlarking' by Less Air (Nicolás Rojas) for his upcoming EP on +Spores. Visuals made using an audioreactive network in TouchDesigner. Produced with an AE Modular Synth and an Arturia Beatstep Pro MIDI controller. Mixing and mastering in FL Studio.

Receptacle, 2023 — A series of drum patterns created exclusively an AE Modular synth (Tangible Waves). Our 3D model orchid was designed in Blender, and imported to TouchDesigner (Derivative) to react to the audio signals from the audio sample.

Remembering the Shannon, 2022 — Audiovisual installation made for Radius (2022) at the Hunt Museum. The piece reflects on the interconnectedness of nature, environment, gender and music. Part of Riverfest and the Eco Show boat programme. Footage, field recordings and interviews documented by Hazel Gardner (Nikon D5100, Zoom H1n). Production, mixing and mastering by Nicolás Rojas Sanabria (AE Modular, Arturia Beatstep Pro, FL Studio).
